Most of my time goes into AI agents for businesses: assistants that take tasks by voice and read your email and PDFs, calling agents that follow up leads, and automations that move data between tools so nobody copies and pastes all day.

I look at the workflow first and pick the model last. Which steps should the agent own, which should stay with a person, and what happens when something breaks. Get that right and the agent keeps working after the demo.

I also lead a small team of engineers and designers in Pakistan, so what we plan actually gets built.

## Live products

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [**LinkedKraft**](https://linkedkraft.com) | Learns how you write from your old posts, drafts LinkedIn posts in your voice, checks reach before you post, and schedules through LinkedIn's official API. |
| [**Puzzlil**](https://puzzlil.online) | Makes print-ready puzzle books for Amazon KDP. 21 puzzle types, cover and spine worked out, every puzzle checked for one answer. |
